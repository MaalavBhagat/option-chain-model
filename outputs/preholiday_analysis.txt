EXPLORATORY -- pre-holiday session analysis
Hypothesis (stated before looking): expiry-day trades on the last session
before a holiday of 2+ consecutive closed days perform WORSE than other
expiry-day trades. NOT part of the pre-registered baseline; must not
replace or be pooled with outputs/backtest_trades.csv's own summary.

Trading-day calendar source: data/raw/nifty50_daily.csv (yfinance ^NSEI).
NOT the bhavcopy manifest (data/raw/download_manifest.csv) -- the manifest
only covers dates this project chose to download and has real gaps; the
yfinance daily series has one row per actual NSE trading day, no gaps,
which is what's needed to correctly compute the next trading day.
